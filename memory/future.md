🎨 UI/UX Redesign Strategy (Professional Architectural Theme)
To ensure the app does not look like a generic "AI-generated glassmorphism" template, we need to shift towards a professional, CAD-tool aesthetic. Here is the plan to achieve a stunning, high-end design:

Move Away From Glassmorphism: We will replace the floating translucent orbs, intense blur filters, and generic gradients with a clean, high-contrast, structural aesthetic that feels like a professional engineering tool (e.g., AutoCAD, Figma, or Revit).
Typography & Hierarchy: We will switch to a sleek, modern font pairing (like Inter or Roboto Mono for technical data) and ensure strict alignment and spacing. Data (like Vastu scores, dimensions, and warnings) will be presented in crisp, tabular formats rather than rounded pill badges.
True Dark/Light Modes:
Dark Mode: Deep charcoal backgrounds (#121212), subtle border lines (#333), and neon/cyan accent colors for structural lines.
Light Mode: Crisp white backgrounds (#FFFFFF), very light gray panels (#F5F5F5), and dark ink text, mimicking a real blueprint on a drafting table.
Layout Tweaks: We will maintain the current flow (Sidebar for inputs, Center for blueprint, Right for compliance/actions) but tighten it up so it feels like a unified dashboard rather than floating cards.
